Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying from JJ Appliances.

Do your pre-owned appliances come with a warranty? ▾
Yes! Every single unit we sell undergoes a comprehensive 10-point mechanical, electrical, and cooling inspection before being sanitized. All pre-owned units include our store warranty covering parts and labor.
How does local delivery work? ▾
We deliver across Woodstock, Oxford County, London, Kitchener-Waterloo, Cambridge, and Brantford. We offer standard curbside delivery, in-home placement, and hook-up assistance. Use our calculator above to check your exact fee.
Can you haul away my old broken appliance? ▾
Yes! For a small removal fee, our delivery crew will safely disconnect and haul away your old broken refrigerator, stove, or washer and dispose of it responsibly.
Can I inspect and test the machine before buying? ▾
Absolutely! Come visit our Woodstock showroom at Backside Garage, 368 Dundas St. You can open doors, inspect shelves, test burners, and verify temperature readings in person.
